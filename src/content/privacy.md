> **Privacy Commitment:** ExamSimula has taken reasonable precautions as per applicable Indian law (including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and Information Technology (Reasonable security practices and procedures and sensitive personal data or information) Rules, 2011) and implemented industry standards to treat Personal Information as confidential and protect it from unauthorized access or disclosure.

## Scope & Consent

This Privacy Policy applies to all Users (including Students and Educators/Teachers) who access ExamSimula and are required to read and understand this document before submitting any Personal Information or uploading content. This policy governs your use of the ExamSimula website ([https://examsimula.com](https://examsimula.com)), its associated applications, test engines, and services managed by ExamSimula. By submitting Personal Information, you consent to the use and processing of such information in accordance with this Policy.

---

## 1. Usage and Retention of Information

We obtain information you provide when you access and register on the Platform. When you register, you generally provide:

- **Identity & Contact Information:** Your name, age, email address, phone number, password, and educational interests (e.g. JEE Main, JEE Advanced, NEET).
- **Transaction & Checkout Information:** Data relating to exam paper purchases, Razorpay payment order IDs, payment statuses, and educator payout account details (UPI IDs or Razorpay account IDs).
- **Exam Telemetry & Analytics:** Information entered into our system while taking mock tests, submitting options, reviewing answer explanations, time spent per question, and interacting with AI-powered performance recommendations.

We use this Information to provide our Services, analyze and improve platform performance, generate personalized AI focus recommendations, and securely process financial transactions (such as student checkouts and educator payouts). We also use this data to enforce our terms and communicate account notifications to you.

---

## 2. Sharing and Disclosing Personal Information

We use trusted third-party Service Providers to perform essential platform functions on our behalf:

- **Payment Gateways (Razorpay):** For secure student payment processing and automated educator payouts. Financial data is tokenized and processed via PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.
- **Cloud Infrastructure & Databases:** For secure hosting, real-time exam timing sync, and high-availability database storage.
- **Communication Channels:** For critical transactional updates, login verification tokens, and score reports.

These Service Providers have access to your personal information solely to perform their functions. **We do NOT sell, transfer, or rent your personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes without your explicit consent.**

---

## 3. Information Security & Safeguards

We store and process your personal information on secure servers protected by industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3 / 256-bit SSL). We provide physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the Information we process against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

While we endeavor to provide rigorous security, no system can prevent all potential security incidents on the internet. Users are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials and devices.

---

## 4. Public Forums & Marketing Consent

When you use community features or discussion boards on ExamSimula, any information you voluntarily post (comments, solutions, feedback) becomes publicly visible to other participants. Sharing such information is done at your own discretion.

Furthermore, the user consents to the use by ExamSimula of their name, percentile, test rank, and/or verified testimonials for publication, academic leaderboards, and promotion on its online platforms without additional compensation, particularly for recognizing rank-holders in competitive mock examinations.

---

## 5. User Rights, Data Deletion & Withdrawal of Consent

In accordance with the Information Technology (Reasonable security practices and procedures and sensitive personal data or information) Rules, 2011, you have the following rights regarding your Personal Information:

- **Review and Correction:** You may review, access, or request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete Personal Information held by ExamSimula.
- **Withdrawal of Consent & Data Deletion:** You have the right to withdraw your consent for the collection and processing of your Personal Information at any time. You may request permanent deletion of your account, profile data, and test attempt records.
- **Deletion Procedure & Timeline:** To exercise your right to withdraw consent or request complete data deletion, please send an email from your registered email address to our Data Support Officer at [support@examsimula.com](mailto:support@examsimula.com) with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request"*. Upon verification, ExamSimula will erase or permanently anonymize your personal records within **30 business days**, retaining only those transaction logs strictly necessary for statutory tax and legal auditing obligations.
