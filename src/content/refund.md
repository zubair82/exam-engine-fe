> **Overview:** Because ExamSimula provides instantaneous access to digital educational mock tests, questions, and automated analytics, please review our Cancellation and Refund terms below before completing checkout.

## 1. For Students (Exam Paper & Mock Test Purchases)

**Digital Goods Non-Refundable:** All digital goods, including individual mock test papers, full subject bundles, and simulation access licenses, are **strictly non-refundable** once a purchase is completed and the paper content or test engine has been unlocked.

**Order Cancellation:** Once an order is placed through the Razorpay payment gateway, access is immediately provisioned to your account. Orders cannot be cancelled, reversed, or swapped for other tests after payment authorization.

---

## 2. Failed Transactions & Debited Funds

If a payment transaction fails during checkout but your bank account, card, or UPI app was debited, the full amount will be automatically reversed and credited back to your original source payment method by the payment gateway (Razorpay) within **5 to 7 business days**.

---

## 3. For Educators & Creators (Marketplace Earnings)

Platform fees, payment gateway interchange charges, and applicable transaction taxes (GST/TDS) deducted from gross student test sales are non-refundable. Payouts, once dispatched to an educator's verified bank or UPI account, cannot be cancelled or recalled.

---

## 4. Account Termination

Users may close or delete their account at any time by reaching out to support. Account closure does not entitle the user to a refund of previously purchased digital papers, historical access, or unused platform services.

---

## 5. Payment Dispute & Support Escalation

If you experience duplicate debits for the exact same paper, or face a critical platform disruption during an active timed test that prevents the paper from generating, please notify our support team within **48 hours** of the transaction. You must include your **Razorpay Payment ID** in your request.

For billing help or payment queries, our desk responds within 24-48 business hours.
